Anthropic extended 50% higher weekly limits through August 19 for Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise seat users. On the surface it's a small usage-limit announcement. Underneath, it signals something bigger.

---

## Limits are becoming part of the product

Claude Code is becoming a tool people use continuously, not occasionally. When usage climbs this high, limits, model access, context, and pricing stop being backend details — they become part of the actual product experience.

---

## Where this is heading

With Opus 4.8, Sonnet 5, and increasingly autonomous workflows shipping in quick succession, it feels less like waiting for one big AI moment and more like these tools quietly changing every few weeks — and the usage limits are just the visible edge of that shift.
