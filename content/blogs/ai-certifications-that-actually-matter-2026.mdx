Amazon, JPMorgan Chase, and Deloitte have all listed AWS or Google AI certifications under "preferred qualifications" in job postings over the past year — a real shift from a few years ago.

---

## The market narrowed, it didn't disappear

The market didn't stop caring about certifications; it narrowed what it cares about to AI. A PMP or a generic "leadership" badge doesn't pull the same weight anymore, but an AI-related credential signals you can be trusted with these tools unsupervised.

---

## A useful check

One useful check before chasing a certification: search the exact certification name on a job board. If it shows up across real listings, it's a signal worth acting on. If not, no amount of marketing changes the fact that hiring managers aren't screening for it.
